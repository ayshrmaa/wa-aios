## 1. What we provide
The AI Operations System: AI Receptionist, Reporting Dashboard, AI Customer Service, Lead Follow-Up,
Appointment Reminders, WhatsApp and Instagram Automation, and the Review & Reputation System, as
configured during onboarding and recorded in the Onboarding Schedule attached to this agreement.

The Onboarding Schedule is binding. It records which services you receive and, where your booking
system limits what is possible, what those limits are.

## 2. Fee
CHF {{monthly_fee}} per month, invoiced monthly in advance. **There is no setup fee.** The build is
included in the monthly fee.

## 3. Term
Minimum term of six months from the go-live date, not the signature date. Thereafter it continues
monthly until either party gives 30 days' written notice.

Because there is no setup fee, the build cost is recovered across the minimum term. If you terminate
during the minimum term other than for our material breach, the remaining months of the minimum term
fall due.

## 4. What we need from you
The access and information listed in the Onboarding Schedule. We cannot begin the build without it,
and the go-live date moves by however long it is outstanding.

You are responsible for the accuracy of the service menu, pricing, and opening hours you give us. The
assistant repeats what you tell it.

## 5. Your obligations as data controller
You are the data controller for your clients' data. You agree to publish a privacy policy covering the
processing described in the attached DPA, and you acknowledge that the AI recording disclosure plays on
every call and may not be disabled.

## 6. What we do not promise
The assistant answers calls, books appointments, and follows up. It is software, and it will
occasionally mishandle a call. We monitor, correct, and improve it as part of the fee.

We do not guarantee a specific number of bookings, a specific reduction in no-shows, or a specific
revenue outcome. Figures in any audit report or proposal are estimates based on the information you
gave us, and are illustrative.

## 7. Service levels
- Support response within one business day.
- Faults that stop calls being answered: we begin work within four business hours.
- If the assistant is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours, that month is credited pro rata.
- Planned maintenance outside salon opening hours where possible.

## 8. Call forwarding
You control your phone line. Forwarding to the assistant is set up together at go-live and you can
remove it at any time. Removing it stops the service; it does not end this agreement or the fee.

## 9. Intellectual property
The system, workflows, prompts, and dashboard remain ours. You have a licence to use them for the term.
Your data — client records, appointments, recordings, message history — is yours, and we return or
delete it on termination as set out in the DPA.

## 10. Liability
Neither party excludes liability for death, personal injury, or fraud. Otherwise our total liability is
capped at the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim. Neither party is liable for lost profits
or lost business.
