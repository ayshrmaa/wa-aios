## What we collect
When you call, message, or book with us we collect your name, phone number, email address (if you give
one), your appointment history and service preferences, and any notes relevant to your appointment.

**Calls to our main line are answered by an automated assistant and are recorded.** You are told this
at the start of every call. If you would rather not speak to an automated assistant or be recorded, say
so and you will be put through to a member of staff.

We also collect messages you send us on WhatsApp or Instagram, and any feedback or rating you give us
after an appointment.

## Why we use it
- To book, change, and remind you about appointments
- To answer your questions
- To follow up on an enquiry you made
- To ask for feedback after your visit
- To understand how our salon is performing

## Legal basis
Performance of our agreement with you (bookings and reminders), our legitimate interest in running and
improving the salon, and your consent for marketing messages. You can withdraw consent at any time
without affecting your bookings.

## Who we share it with
We use service providers to run these systems, including our automation partner Work Artificial and the
platforms listed in our processing agreement with them. They act on our instructions and may not use
your data for their own purposes. We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it
Call recordings for 90 days. Transcripts for 12 months. Appointment records for the duration of our
relationship plus 24 months.

## Your rights
You may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it.
Contact {{salon_email}}. You may also complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information
Commissioner (FDPIC).

## Marketing messages
Every marketing message includes a way to stop them. Appointment reminders are not marketing and
continue unless you cancel your appointment.
